Evidence-first opportunity research

Import an array of business ideas with public evidence URLs and explicit 0–5 research ratings. This tool ranks hypotheses; it does not search the web or verify links automatically.

Qualification gates

  1. Identify a specific buyer and painful recurring workflow.
  2. Find evidence customers already spend money.
  3. Document competitors and a meaningful product gap.
  4. Estimate acquisition costs, build effort and margins.
  5. Interview potential customers before committing funds.

No proof, no “verified opportunity.”

Research priorities

Start with narrow B2B workflows, developer utilities and AI infrastructure. Avoid generic clones, speculative transaction volume, and unverified revenue forecasts.

Score weights: pain 25%, recurrence 20%, willingness to pay 20%, feasibility 15%, distribution 10%, differentiation 10%.

A high score is a hypothesis ranking, not evidence of actual sales.
